POD Studio AI

The ultimate AI-powered screenplay and storyboard pipeline.

Overview

POD Studio is an advanced AI extension inside TubeCLI designed to automate the process of translating and adapting literature into professional screenplays and storyboards. It uses AI agents to break down raw text, extract characters, map scenes, and generate detailed video production prompts.

Core Pipeline Workflow

1

Raw Content & Organization

Start by creating a Project (Ad Campaign) and adding instances for each Episode. Paste your source material (novel chapters, rough ideas, or raw script) into the 01 Raw Content tab.

2

AI Rewrite

Switch to the 02 AI Rewrite tab. The Script Rewriter agent will transform your raw text into a standard cinematic screenplay format with clear character dialogues, stage directions, and narrative flow. You can use the chat interface on the right to instruct the AI (e.g., "Make the dialogue more campaigntic").

3

Extract Models & Products & Scenes

In the 03 Extract tab, click Extract Models & Products & Scenes. The AI will analyze the script and generate a database of all characters (roles, appearances, personalities) and locations. The system automatically deduplicates existing characters across episodes to maintain continuity.

4

Storyboard Breakdown

Finally, open the 04 Storyboard tab. The Storyboard Breaker agent divides your screenplay into actionable 10-15 second camera shots. Each shot lists the exact camera angle, character action, dialogue, BGM, Sound Effects, and auto-generates English Image Prompts and Video Prompts ready for AI generation tools (like Midjourney, Runway, or Pika).

Configuration & Settings

POD Studio requires a configured AI Provider to function. While it inherits global TubeCLI settings by default, you can override them:

  • Click the Settings icon in the top right corner.
  • Language: Set the target language for your scripts (e.g., Vietnamese, English, Korean).
  • Global AI Override: By default, the studio uses the active API key from TubeCLI (e.g., DeepSeek, Gemini, OpenAI). You can disable "Use global AI settings" to manually set a specific API endpoint and key solely for the Studio.
  • Agent Parameters: Adjust the Temperature for each agent (higher = more creative, lower = more deterministic).

Advanced Tip: Writing the Next Episode (Ep 2, 3...)

The system is designed to maintain continuity for the entire series using a shared database of Models & Products and Scenes for each Project. To write Episode 2 based on Episode 1:

  • Step 1: In the left sidebar, click + Add Episode under your project name to create Ep 2. Click on Ep 2 to select it.
  • Step 2: Paste any raw content for the new episode into the 01 Raw Content tab. If you want the AI to freely generate the next part without a source, leave it blank.
  • Step 3: Switch to 02 AI Rewrite. In the chat panel on the right, instruct the AI: "This is episode 2. Using the existing characters, continue the story from the end of episode 1. The male lead should discover the truth..."
  • Step 4: The backend automatically provides the AI with the list of Models & Products & Scenes from Episode 1. The AI will utilize this context to write a seamless Episode 2 without breaking plot continuity.
